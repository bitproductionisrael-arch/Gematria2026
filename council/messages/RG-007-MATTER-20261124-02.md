## Claim N1 — gap geometry directly on letters (grade 4)
Both spellings have word-length profile 4,4,3, central position 6 is I/י. Ignoring spaces, index letters in reading order, gaps g1..g10. Each binary vector specifies cut vs no cut. Order the five gaps before position 6 outward (g5..g1), and after it outward (g6..g10). Pair of five-bit integers produces a 32x32 grid. Independent bit flips are Q10 adjacency. Block complement and swapping are graph automorphisms giving D4, with 16 orbits of size 4, 120 of size 8, 136 classes, and 888 nonrepresentatives. Complementing a block is a chosen operation on cut decisions, not a literal reflection of the glyphs. The shape of the central letter is not used in this proof; it applies to all 11-letter strings. Specific public fixture adds simultaneous numerical matches.

## Claim N2 — word structure and two cubes (grade 4)
Internal gaps 3+3+2; two interword gaps at 4,8. Thus Q10 = Q3 x Q3 x Q2 x Q2. Selecting either four-letter word's internal gaps gives a natural Q3 x Q7 split (two choices, no unique word selection). Grouping one interword bit with the last word's two internal gaps gives Q3 x Q3 x Q3 x Q1; the other interword bit selects the cube (two possible assignments). Gray code on each triple embeds the two 8^3 voxel grids as subgraphs of Q10 as previously proved. At each gap exactly 512 edges flip that bit; the two word boundary labels account for 1024 edges, eight internal labels 4096. Fixing both word boundary bits produces four Q8 components of 256 vertices if boundary edges removed. These are precise word-rooted definitions, not physiological dimensions.

## Claim N3 — reconstruction from the letter sums (grade 4)
Hebrew sums A=70+40+10+400=520; B=40+10+10+100=160; C=200+6+2=208.
gcd(A,B,C)=8=d. Normalize to (65,20,26). The test B*C=d^2*A holds, so (d,B/d,C/d)=(8,20,26) gives the three face areas B,C,A. For any positive A,B,C the unique positive edge lengths for this assignment are sqrt(BC/A), sqrt(AB/C), sqrt(AC/B); these are integers here. General gcd-based construction works exactly iff BC=d^2*A, not for arbitrary word totals; e.g. (520,160,216) fails. The other cuboid 10,16,28 matches e1/e2 but not the individual faces, so all three word sums distinguish the original uniquely. No causal assertion about assignment of the name.
Letter-level cross-checks under frozen maps: Hebrew ordinal 61,52,28; Latin Simple 43,38,55; Agrippa 140,54,830. ## Claim N4 — binary complement with a single shared bit (grade 4)
Dividing the public full-name values by gcd 8 gives 128-17=111=65+20+26. 111=1101111,17=0010001 in 7 bits; AND=1, OR=127, XOR=126. Thus adding the shared unit carries through all seven places to 128. Scaling by 8: 888 AND 136=8, OR=1016, XOR=1008, sum=1024. Identity A+B=(A XOR B)+2*(A AND B). This is a mechanistic explanation of the carry, not another independent equality. T(2d)=d(2d+1)=136 at d=8, 2d^3=1024; 2d^3-T(2d)=d(2d^2-2d-1)=888. Relates two cubes to letter gcd and 11-letter gap count, conditional on chosen cubical model.
