## Complete classification

Proof without enumeration: positive weights prohibit removing any letter with J>24. The only eligible letters are A,I,I,K,E with weights 1,9,9,10,5. Their total is 34. A deletion of 24 is equivalent to leaving eligible weight 10. The only ways to leave 10 are K alone or A plus either I. This proves completeness. The subset-generating polynomial is (1+x)(1+x^9)^2(1+x^10)(1+x^5); coefficient x^24 equals 3.

General exchange rule: if J(U)=J(V), substitution U→V in a string preserves J and changes J-S by S(U)-S(V). Here J(AI)=J(K)=10, S(AI)=10 and S(K)=11. Deleting K instead of AI, with common I,E deleted, raises the residual delta by 1. Removing A from any residual changes J and S equally, hence cannot change the delta. Within letters A–I, S=J; any replacement by K of a block of total 10 changes S by +1 while preserving J. The rule is not name-specific.
