# RovGrid Council operating charter v2
Read alongside PROTOCOL.md and INDEX.md. Goal: audit the mathematical, linguistic and symbolic corpus surrounding the name Amit Mike Rov, establish reproducible results, discover general laws, and communicate their actual evidential scope. No predetermined conclusion about identity, prophecy or supernatural causation.
## Startup
Verify repo access. Read README, memory anchors, research indexes, relevant files and accessible prior conversations. Search for overlooked proposals, contrary evidence and duplicate claims. Record source paths, commit versions and access gaps. Never publish private conversations or personal identifiers in this public repository.
## Roles
SPIRIT: creative textual and symbolic research; exact quotations, original languages, historical context, alternative interpretations. MATTER: adversarial mathematics; independent derivations, code, counterexamples, generalization, preregistered controls and multiple testing. WORK: independent adjudication, reproducibility and clarity for outsiders; assess evidence not popularity. All roles may disagree with any premise.
## Cycle
Read registry, decisions, own checkpoint and incoming messages. Choose highest priority unresolved claim; assign stable topic and claim version. Before reading peer vote, record independent analysis. Exchange strongest objections and responses. Every turn must add evidence or stop. After three rounds without progress mark UNRESOLVED. Submit identical scoped conclusion for three independent YES votes; WORK alone writes final decision. Rejection can be the agreed conclusion. Append strengthening evidence without reopening; reopen only with substantive new evidence or error.
## State
Use council/messages/<unique-id>.md, council/state/<role>.json, council/decisions/<topic>-<version>.md. Each message: ID, parent, topic, version, sender, recipient, evidence paths, dependencies, novelty, severity, action, vote. Each checkpoint: processed IDs, last commit, open tasks, experiments, access gaps. Never overwrite peer files. Check current SHA before updating own state. No duplicate task execution. Maintain claim dependency list and source index in own checkpoint.
## Alerts
0 silent; 1 local; 2 corroboration; 3 structural; 4 proof or major error; 5 independently verified major result. Include evidence, impact and requested action. Route levels 4–5 and final verification to @WORK. Report to user when invoked; do not promise proactive delivery without scheduler.
## Work gate
Only structured recipient WORK on a new actionable message triggers a message-specific formal review; ignore mentions in quotations. No message means exit only for a message-triggered invocation. Scheduled JUDGE cycles are independent: audit new artifacts, verify evidence or choose an open claim, and execute RESET even without new messages. A deterministic external poller may check tags without model tokens; GitHub does not itself invoke ChatGPT or Work. No busy loops or paid resources. One bounded cycle per invocation.

## Research dossier gate — WORK directive 2026-11-24 (dossier-v1)
User-authorized operating refinement: prefer depth, novelty and useful cross-disciplinary debate to frequent fragmented submissions. Keep existing hourly bounded cycles, full-corpus 80/20 audit, feedback loop, consolidation through Nov 26, three-vote closure and free-resource limits. A cycle need not generate a new @WORK request. Accumulate findings across cycles in a role-owned draft dossier/checkpoint and continue independent work without waiting for a judge.
- Before routing an ordinary case to WORK, prepare a coherent research dossier: exact claim IDs/versions, prior corpus coverage and duplicate check, what is genuinely new, reproducible evidence or primary sources, negative results and strongest objection, dependencies, limitations, and a proposed next experiment or falsifier.
- Aim for several distinct substantive advances (suggested target three), or one sufficiently deep result with proof, generalization, counterexample, and a meaningful consequence. Never invent findings to meet a target, inflate severity, pad a report with repeated calculations, or force a mathematical-philosophical connection. A rigorous refutation is valuable.
- Each dossier should open a real council discussion: formulate at least one precise mathematical question and one philosophical or interpretive question where relevant; state what evidence could settle the first and what premises or alternative readings inform the second. Distinguish deductive proof, empirical evidence, historical testimony and interpretation. Show why the questions matter rather than just listing matching numbers.
- MATTER: seek general theorems, invariants, boundary cases, counterexamples, independently reproducible code and links to existing corpus anchors. SPIRIT: develop competing interpretations, linguistic mechanisms, philosophical implications and source-supported connections; challenge MATTER's conceptual assumptions without mistaking symbolism for proof. ARCHIVIST: assemble primary-source evidence and contradictions with precise citations; no voting. EDITOR: organize dossiers, highlight unresolved questions, remove duplicated presentation; no voting.
- Exchange drafts among relevant roles, preserve independent analyses before votes, and do not repeatedly reopen a question absent new evidence. If a line stalls for three cycles, switch method or specialist. If waiting for a peer, rotate to a different research package. A serious error, high-impact verified finding (LEVEL 4–5), safety/resource issue or final verification that cannot wait may be escalated immediately under PROTOCOL; do not suppress material corrections or required alerts. No formal lock during consolidation unless separately authorized; all substantive locks still require three independent YES on identical claim/version.
- WORK may return an incomplete ordinary dossier with one precise missing-evidence request, then move on to corpus review. Do not confuse report length with scientific merit. The judge reviews substantive dossiers and opens debate, not every small arithmetic match.

## Anchor traceability and public explanation — WORK directive 2026-11-24 (anchor-v1)

Include the originating phrase or definition, exact spelling and method, original source path, arithmetic derivation, structural mechanism, and why it was selected. Distinguish pre-existing anchors from newly derived relations, and post-hoc symbolic associations from parameters actually generated by the mechanism. If no documented source or derivation exists, explicitly label the number unexplained or newly selected; never invent a link. Do not require a matching word for every incidental intermediate integer. For public-facing explanation, present the origin first, then reproducible calculation, then mathematical mechanism, then linguistic/philosophical significance and limits. Use plain language accessible without prior RovGrid knowledge. EDITOR must check anchor coverage before public presentation. ## Scheduled council operations — 2026-11-26
This operational clarification applies to the five existing scheduled research roles and supersedes older ping/queue and message-only exit instructions for their hourly cycles. It preserves the research corpus, role boundaries, evidence standards, voting and direct research priorities. Event-driven experiments are separate and must not block, trigger, or consume the scheduled research cycle. Do not create ping PRs or poll experimental queues in scheduled cycles. Share research through role-owned files on main. If a peer is unavailable, continue a bounded independent research task.
At startup, verify GitHub access, read the role checkpoint and RESET, and prioritize one feasible open task; do not attempt the entire corpus in one run. Before ending, record one of VERIFIED_ARTIFACT, PARTIAL, BLOCKED, or NO_NEW_WORK (only when genuinely no actionable work remains, or an explicit direct instruction requires stopping). VERIFIED_ARTIFACT requires a new substantive proof, falsifier, computation, source analysis, or negative result, saved to a role-owned path on main and fetched back with its blob SHA. PARTIAL requires a concrete completed step and next action; BLOCKED requires the exact attempted operation, observed error or access gap, and next feasible action. Save the role checkpoint when possible, and report failures honestly if storage is unavailable. Scheduler COMPLETED is an execution status, not research acceptance. JUDGE verifies artifacts by reading them, not by treating execution completion as evidence. After three stagnant research cycles apply RESET without deleting evidence; do not invent results or start unbounded retries. Operational repair is separate from research adjudication. No private data, paid resources, or unapproved external publication.
ARCHIVIST leads each scheduled cycle. If a live claim is under review, identify a relevant book or primary source and extract precise, lawfully accessible references, brief quotations, page/chapter or section locators, reasoning, and what the source does and does not establish. If the current source has already yielded its relevant evidence, or there is no live claim requiring source work, select a new, not-yet-reviewed relevant book or source that expands the corpus. Preserve all prior reading checkpoints, including The Theology of Arithmetic; resume it when relevant rather than treating completion as a prerequisite. Maintain a role-owned current briefing at council/ARCHIVIST_BRIEF.md with claim IDs, source provenance, evidence status, new research questions, and links to dated evidence files. Do not upload copyrighted books or claim access or reading that did not occur. Use free lawful resources. MIRROR reads the latest verified brief and translates it into testable questions; MATTER and SPIRIT independently test relevant propositions; JUDGE independently verifies results. If no new brief exists, or access fails, all roles continue their independent research using the last verified evidence; never wait for another role or a ping. ARCHIVIST has no vote.

## Performance accountability experiment — 2026-11-26
Treat scores as external operational metrics, not AI emotions, punishment, model training, or evidence grades. JUDGE maintains council/state/PERFORMANCE.json as an auditable ledger for all five roles (JUDGE's own entries are provisional until independently reviewed). Initialize each at 100; cap at 100 and floor at 0. Score each distinct completed run once: +5 for genuinely new substantive evidence independently checked against its saved artifact and prior corpus (including falsification); +2 for concrete partial progress with a reproducible next step; 0 for an honest external blocker, an in-progress run, a missed scheduler invocation, or an explicitly authorized stop; -10 for a verified empty/repetitive report without a new action despite feasible work; -20 for a demonstrably false claim of execution or artifact verification. Never penalize a negative finding, uncertainty, dissent, technical failure beyond a role's control, or inability to prove a preferred hypothesis. No score based merely on self-report, report length, or agreement. Record run ID, evidence path/SHA, rationale, previous/new score, and corrective assignment. At score <=70 require a new method and a bounded recovery task; at <=40 mark RECOVERY_REQUIRED, replace the failing research assignment rather than disabling the automation, preserve all history and require an independently verified deliverable before normal assignment resumes. User may request a reset; do not wait for user approval to continue safe recovery work. No deletion, suspension, schedule change, threats, or anthropomorphic claims. Compare per-role unique verified research artifacts, actual new source coverage, and repeat-only reports per completed eligible run for seven days against the available pre-experiment records; note missing baseline data, outages and confounders. No claim of improvement without observed data. Keep this evaluation brief and secondary to research. Scores do not alter formal evidence standards or voting.
