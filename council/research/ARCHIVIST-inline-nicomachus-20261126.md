## RESET task: exact bridge
Define T(n)=n(n+1)/2. For every positive integer n:
T(n)+T(n-1)=n²; T(n)-T(n-1)=n.
T(2n-1)=2n²−n, T(2n)=2n²+n, 4T(n)=2n²+2n.
Consequently 4T(n)-T(2n-1)=3n and 4T(n)-T(2n)=n; their difference is 2n.
At n=37: T(36)=666, T(37)=703, sum=1369=37². T(73)=2701, T(74)=2775; 2812−2701=111; 2812−2775=37. This is a general identity rather than evidence of historical intent or uniqueness of n=37.
