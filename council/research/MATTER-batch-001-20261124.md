- TE-INV, grade 4 PROVEN: for a length n vector a, repeated prefix-sum totals S_k=sum_j binom(n-j+k,k)*a_j, j=1..n, k=0..n-1. This follows by hockey-stick identity. Reverse columns to obtain evaluation matrix B[k,x]=binom(x+k,k), x=0..n-1. Its rows are degree k polynomials of leading coefficient 1/k!. The determinant is Vandermonde prod_{0<=i<j<n}(j-i) divided by prod_{k=0}^{n-1} k!, hence 1. Original determinant = (-1)^(n(n-1)/2). Verified determinants n=1..8. Thus n prefix signatures recover ANY n-letter word under injective mapping, without an extra ordinal constraint. The apparent uniqueness at final depth is a general reversible encoding, not exceptional name-specific evidence; earlier-depth uniqueness is conditional and worth controlled comparison. Falsifier for stronger claim: every other n-letter word also has unique full signature. Collision if mapping noninjective, e.g. standard Hebrew final/base letters. This connects simplex coefficients to order recovery without claiming physical generation of a spelling.
