## Primary text witnesses actually retrieved (English translations, not manuscript autopsy)
- Arabic Gospel of the Infancy of the Saviour, Alexander Walker, Ante-Nicene Fathers VIII (1886), §36, https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/0806.htm : “completed seven years from His birth”. Clay animals and birds; parents subsequently warn children away. Age seven does not itself mark a universal transition to benevolence.
- Infancy Gospel of Thomas, Syriac, Tony Burke translation, §§11–13, https://www.tonyburke.ca/infancy-gospel-of-thomas/the-infancy-gospel-of-thomas-syriac/ : “when Jesus was seven years old” (§11); water in cloak; §12 one measure of wheat yields 100 cors, distributed to villagers; §13 explicitly eight years old, wood length miracle. §12 has no fresh age marker.
- Old Latin tradition, Pseudo-Matthew pars altera, A. Walker 1873 translation, §§33–34, https://www.tonyburke.ca/infancy-gospel-of-thomas/the-infancy-gospel-of-thomas-old-latin/ : “Jesus was six years old”; same water episode, subsequent harvest three kors, not 100. This is a concrete double variant (age and yield), not an independent corroboration of seven.
- Late Latin, §11, https://www.tonyburke.ca/infancy-gospel-of-thomas/the-infancy-gospel-of-thomas-late-latin/ : water episode at six. Parallel Walker text Latin form ch.9–10 https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/0846.htm : six and harvest 100 pecks. Do not count translations as independent manuscripts.
- Arabic IGT (distinct from Arabic Gospel of Infancy), Slavomír Céplö translation in Burke, The Infancy Gospel of Thomas in the Syriac Tradition, Gorgias 2017, pp.229–243, §§1,7–9, https://www.tonyburke.ca/infancy-gospel-of-thomas/the-arabic-infancy-gospel-of-thomas/ : clay birds at five, water at seven carried in hand, not cloak; later teacher dies. This is a direct counterexample to treating seven as irreversible moral transition.
- Greek S §2, https://www.tonyburke.ca/infancy-gospel-of-thomas/infancy-gospel-of-thomas-greek-s/ : “The boy Jesus was five years old” at clay sparrows episode. Greek A §11 in Burke online translation has seven for water, §12 harvest 100 measures, §13 eight: https://www.tonyburke.ca/infancy-gospel-of-thomas/infancy-gospel-of-thomas-greek-a/ . Edition-dependent; compare Walker's versions before generalizing.

## Disposition and handoff

No primary witness here connects these ages to David's reign. Latin return to Bethlehem at seven remains unverified.
