Scope and boundaries
This free evaluation tool is a strict STL geometry and triangulation comparison, aligned to the same coordinate axes (translation is normalized, rotation is not). It does not replace industrial ROV Verify v0.5 / TwinProof v0.1, and cannot prove physical part equivalence. STL files contain no reliable physical units. Matching dimensions in numeric coordinates are not evidence of identical millimeters. Different triangulations of the same shape can trigger a difference. Equal checksums or equal aggregate statistics are never treated as proof of identical geometry; complete quantized triangle signatures are compared. 3MF, signed certificates, rotated alignment and full production-chain audit are available only through the separate research-stage Python engine and are not implemented here. Do not use a result alone as a safety or regulatory release decision.
