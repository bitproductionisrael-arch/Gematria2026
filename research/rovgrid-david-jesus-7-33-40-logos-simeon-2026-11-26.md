## A. David: chronology, covenant, Jerusalem
2 Sam 2:1–11: Judah anoints David in Hebron; Abner installs Saul's son Ish-bosheth at Mahanaim over the other tribes. Jerusalem is still Jebusite. 2 Sam 3:21: David sends Abner away in peace (וישלח דוד את אבנר וילך בשלום); Joab kills Abner; 2 Sam 4: David punishes the killers of Ish-bosheth. 2 Sam 5:3: elders covenant with David in Hebron, anoint him over Israel; unity precedes the conquest of Jerusalem (5:6–10). Distinguish peaceful accession to united Israel from military conquest of the city. 2 Sam 24:23 calls Araunah “the king” in one reading; Radak and Metzudat David interpret him as previous Jebusite king, not an unambiguous historical identification. Jerusalem's location as shared capital is an explanatory interpretation.
2 Sam 5:4: בן שלשים שנה דוד במלכו ארבעים שנה מלך. Age 30, total 40, implied death ~70. 5:5 specifies 7 years AND 6 months in Hebron, 33 in Jerusalem; 1 Kings 2:11 uses rounded seven and thirty-three: והימים אשר מלך דוד על ישראל ארבעים שנה בחברון מלך שבע שנים ובירושלם מלך שלשים ושלש שנים. Thus 7+33=40 in Kings, not exact duration in Samuel. Around age 37 on transition.
1 Chr 12:39 (Hebrew versification; check English 12:38): לב אחד להמליך את דויד. לב=32, אחד=13, לב אחד=45=אדם. 2 Sam 8:15: ויהי דוד עשה משפט וצדקה לכל עמו. 1 Chr 22:9 explicitly connects Solomon's name with peace and quiet: כי שלמה יהיה שמו ושלום ושקט אתן על ישראל בימיו. Ecclesiastes 1:1: דברי קהלת בן דוד מלך בירושלם. Phrase constructed for comparison, not a biblical verse: שלום עמית קהלת. Conventional regular for שלמה=375 vs שלום=376; do not equate their numeric values.

## C. Isaiah, Jeremiah, Davidic expectation
Isaiah 7:14: הנה העלמה הרה וילדת בן וקראת שמו עמנו אל. 7+7=14=דוד is an association with later verse numbering. Matthew 1:22–23 explicitly cites Isaiah in its birth narrative. Isaiah 9:5–6 in Hebrew numbering: child born, שר שלום, David's throne, peace without end. Rashi relates to Hezekiah; Christian tradition relates to Jesus; separate interpretations. Jeremiah 33:15–17: righteous Davidic branch, judgment and justice, salvation of Judah, secure Jerusalem and continuity of David's throne. Luke 1:32–33 explicitly promises David's throne, rule over Jacob and no end to kingdom, with latter in verse 33. Verse/chapter numbering postdates original texts; association not evidence of authorship.

## D. Jesus, 7/8/33/40, and the rejected weaker comparisons
Luke 3:23: Jesus was about 30 at start of ministry; David was 30 when he became king. Age 33 at death is traditional estimate, not explicit canonical text. Luke 2:42: age twelve at temple; not equate age with twelve tribes. Both connected to Bethlehem in biblical narratives. Noncanonical infancy traditions: Arabic Infancy Gospel ch.36 associates age seven with clay figures/animals and birds brought to life; Syriac Infancy Thomas traditions associate seven with carrying water in garment and extraordinary crop; Latin recension alleged return to Bethlehem at seven. Prior conversation mentioned 2015 article “The Moral Development of the Boy Jesus in the Infancy Gospel of Thomas” and transition from cursing to blessing. Exact recensions, chapter numbering, author and article details REQUIRE ARCHIVIST verification, not accepted as independently established; user rejected age-seven-only as target. No invented direct equivalence of childhood ages and reign durations.
Stronger explicit sequence: Leviticus 12:2–4, seven days of maternal impurity, circumcision on eighth day, 33 days of blood purification; 7+33=40; day 8 lies within the 40, not an extra elapsed day. Luke 2:21–24 reports circumcision/name Jesus on day eight and temple presentation in Jerusalem at completion of purification according to Moses. Luke does not restate 7 and 33 independently; those are derived from Leviticus. The law applies to any male birth, not unique to Jesus. Distinctive narrative sequence is naming Jesus, temple presentation, Simeon's promise before death (2:26), blessing and proclamation of salvation (2:30), light to nations (2:32), Davidic throne promise (1:32–33). The David parallel is seven years local rule + 33 united rule, not a parallel historical duration. David enters Jerusalem early in second phase; Jesus presented at completion of purification. Do not misstate them as identical timing.
