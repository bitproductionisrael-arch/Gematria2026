# Foundational library — 11 works
Reading programme, not validated findings. Prefer public-domain editions and lawful open access. Do not upload copyrighted modern translations without permission.
1. Pseudo-Iamblichus, Theology of Arithmetic — monad through decad; Greek original Theologumena arithmeticae (Ast 1817); Waterfield translation reference only.
2. Nicomachus, Introduction to Arithmetic — figurate numbers, ratios. Open access full edition https://www.fulcrum.org/concern/monographs/zs25xc31c ; reproducible classroom edition https://stoicheiamath.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Nicomachus-Introduction-to-Arithmetic-Student-Edition.pdf
3. Theon of Smyrna, Mathematics Useful for Understanding Plato — Hiller Greek edition 1878; ratios and side-diagonal numbers.
4. Plato, Timaeus — 35b–36b world soul ratios; 53c–56c solids; public domain Bury edition, catalogue https://scholiaproject.org/
5. Euclid, Elements — books VII–IX arithmetic, XIII solids; https://mathcs.clarku.edu/~djoyce/java/elements/elements.html
6. Sefer Yetzirah — 32 paths, 22 letters and 10 sefirot, permutations; public domain original/Westcott edition https://archive.org/details/sepheryetzirahb00rittgoog
7. Hayyim Vital, Etz Chaim — circles and line, surrounding/inner light; https://www.sefaria.org/Sefer_Etz_Chaim.1.2.5
8. Sulbasutras — ritual altar transformations, diagonal approximation; locate lawful Thibaut historical edition.
9. Zhu Xi, Yixue qimeng — binary doubling; https://www2.kenyon.edu/Depts/Religion/Fac/Adler/Writings/Qimeng-3.pdf (link only; verify permissions).
10. Proclus, Elements of Theology — unity and procession; public-domain Taylor edition https://neoplatonic-library.org/
11. Kepler, Harmonices Mundi — harmonic ratios, geometry and cosmology; locate public-domain 1619 Latin scan.
