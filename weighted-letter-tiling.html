GEOMETRY LAB · Weighted Letter Tiling← Geometry Lab

A geometric construction, not a photograph: one point per English ordinal unit of each letter. The preset follows the approved rule: ROV = rows 1–10; MIKE = full rows 11–13 plus both endpoints of row 14; AMIT = the other 12 points of row 14 and rows 15–16. Letters are placed row-major, left to right within each named region. The site checks every count before rendering or exporting. This exact boundary is the documented T16 preset, not an automatic theorem for other names.
